Our Story

Made slowly, on purpose.

ROOH began in a single room in Ahmedabad with two looms and a conviction: that clothes carry the hands that make them.

2023

Two looms, one idea

We started with a simple frustration — that "contemporary Indian fashion" so often meant either untouched tradition or diluted trend, with nothing sitting comfortably between. ROOH set out to find that middle ground: heritage textiles, cut for the way people actually get dressed.

Every collection begins with the fabric first — hand-block prints, natural dyes, handloom weaves — and the silhouette follows.

Today

Small batches, by design

We work directly with 120+ weavers, block-printers and tailors across Gujarat and Rajasthan, producing in small, deliberate runs rather than chasing volume. When a piece sells out, it's often gone for good — and that's intentional.

No overproduction. No dead stock. Just fabric that's meant to be lived in, season after season.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Hands, not machines

Every print is block-stamped and every weave is set by hand — imperfections included, because they're proof of the maker.

Fair, direct partnerships

We work without middlemen, paying artisan partners directly and consistently, collection after collection.

Made to outlast trend

Natural fibres, considered cuts, and a wardrobe philosophy that favours fewer, better pieces.
