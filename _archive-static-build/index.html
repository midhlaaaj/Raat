Our Story

Made slowly, on purpose.

ROOH began in a single room in Ahmedabad with two looms and a conviction: that clothes carry the hands that make them. Every piece is cut in small batches with weavers and block-printers we work with directly, season after season.

No overproduction. No dead stock. Just fabric that's meant to be lived in.
